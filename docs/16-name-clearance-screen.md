# Preliminary name-clearance screen: Parison / `parison`

**Search date:** 6 October 2026  
**Decision:** **GO for a bounded alpha under Parison / `parison`, subject to legal review before material commercial investment or a trademark filing.**  
**Status:** Preliminary screen only. This is not a legal opinion, a comprehensive common-law search, or formal trademark clearance.

## Executive finding

The exact Python distribution name `parison` was unclaimed on PyPI when queried, and the exact npm package name was also unclaimed. The project's GitHub repository exists at `raghavkandpal/parison`; another empty, unrelated public repository named `parison` also exists, so GitHub naming is not exclusive.

The trademark screen found one live exact-word US registration, **PARISON**, owned by Virco Mfg. Corporation for chairs and office furniture in International Class 20 (serial 87164622). That is an important hit, but its stated goods are remote from locally installed data-comparison software and related software services. TMview also returned exact PARISON registrations in unrelated classes and countries, including clothing in Malaysia and India. No exact PARISON hit for software or computer/scientific services in Classes 9 or 42 appeared in the inspected results. Similar-string results included CAPARISON registrations in Class 9 for guitar-related goods and an expired French PARISONIC registration spanning Classes 7, 9, 16, 36, 37 and 42.

The word is not invented solely for this project: WIPO's own 2022 domain decision records that “parison” is a dictionary term in glass-making, and discusses disputed, unproven common-law claims to **PARISON STUDIO** in artisan glass services. The panel found the submitted evidence insufficient to establish trademark rights for that UDRP proceeding. That use is commercially and conceptually remote from data comparison, but it reduces the name's uniqueness and makes broad exclusivity less plausible.

On the evidence available, there is no obvious software/data-comparison conflict requiring an immediate rename. The sensible preliminary result is therefore **GO**, with a narrow product description and without representing the name as legally cleared.

## Scope and method

The intended use screened was:

- the product name **Parison**;
- the Python distribution, import and command name `parison`;
- locally installed software for deterministic comparison of data-pipeline outputs, plus closely related software/documentation services;
- likely relevant Nice classes 9 (downloadable software) and 42 (software/technology services), while checking exact hits in other classes for context.

Queries covered exact `parison` and visible contains/similar-string results. Sources were limited to first-party package/hosting registries, official trademark-office search systems, WIPO materials and registry APIs. Search results are point-in-time observations and may change.

## Trademark and brand searches

### United States — USPTO

**Search performed:** Wordmark search for `parison`, all statuses, in the official USPTO Trademark Search system.

The search returned two records:

1. **PARISON**, live/registered, serial **87164622**, owned by Virco Mfg. Corporation, for “chairs and office furniture,” Class 20.
2. **THE PARISON**, dead/cancelled, serial **76241856**, for newsletters concerning polymer-processing equipment, Class 16.

The live exact-word registration prevents calling the word globally “clear.” Its listed goods are, however, far removed from this project's software. No exact PARISON software or Class 9/42 record appeared in this exact-word USPTO result set. [USPTO Trademark Search](https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/search/search-information), [USPTO record 87164622](https://tsdr.uspto.gov/#caseNumber=87164622&caseSearchType=US_APPLICATION&caseType=DEFAULT&searchType=statusSearch)

## Confusion assessment

Factors supporting continued alpha use:

- no exact PyPI or npm package occupied the `parison` namespace at the time of search;
- the exact live US registration identified covers office furniture, not software;
- the exact international hits visible in TMview were in unrelated goods classes;
- no exact Class 9/42 PARISON record appeared in the inspected USPTO or TMview results;
- the project uses the word suggestively in relation to “comparison,” while the dictionary meaning and known prior uses concern glass manufacturing.

Factors requiring caution:

- an exact live US word registration exists, so the name is not universally free;
- TMview returned hundreds of contains matches, and this screen did not review every record or every goods/services description;
- **PARISON STUDIO** has documented prior commercial/domain use, even though the 2022 WIPO panel found the evidence insufficient for UDRP trademark standing;
- common-law uses, company names, app stores, domains, unindexed filings and phonetic/conceptual variants were not comprehensively searched;
- “Parison” may be heard or mistyped as “comparison,” “Praison,” “Parsion,” “Parisons,” or “Parisonic”; the screen found nearby software/package names such as `parsion`, `parsim` and PraisonAI, although none is an exact match or an identified data-comparison product conflict. [Parsion on PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/parsion/), [Parsim on PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/parsim/), [PraisonAI on PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/PraisonAI/)

## Recommendation and release implication

**Preliminary recommendation: GO** for a bounded, clearly described open-source alpha using **Parison** and `parison`.

Before investing materially in the brand, offering paid services, or filing an application:

1. have qualified trademark counsel run a comprehensive search in the intended launch jurisdictions, including common-law and similar-mark analysis;
2. rerun the official UK IPO and WIPO searches directly, and review all live Class 9/42 contains and phonetic hits in USPTO/TMview rather than only the visible first page;
3. define the actual goods/services wording before assessing class overlap;
4. publish or reserve the PyPI distribution promptly if the alpha will ship there, because today's 404 does not reserve the name;
5. avoid claims such as “trademark cleared” or use of the ® symbol unless and until legally justified.

This screen resolves the repository's **preliminary naming-risk gate** sufficiently for an alpha; it does **not** constitute formal trademark clearance.

## Limitations

- Searches reflect registry state observed on 6 October 2026 and can become stale immediately.
- Search-system matching, transliteration, coverage and status data can be incomplete.
- The WIPO interactive search and a separately verifiable UK IPO result set were unavailable in this environment.
- The TMview review was limited to visible first-page results and facets from a 416-result contains search.
- No comprehensive common-law, corporate-name, app-store, social-handle or domain-registration search was performed.
- No legal analysis was performed concerning jurisdiction, priority, descriptiveness, dilution, consent, coexistence or actual marketplace use.
