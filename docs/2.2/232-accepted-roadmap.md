# 2.2 accepted roadmap

Date: 23 October 2026  
Status: six capabilities released in 2.2.0; original acceptance decision preserved

This decision supersedes the scope targets in research documents 229 and 230. Preserve those documents and the [independent review](231-independent-feature-review.md) as decision evidence.

## Six substantial capabilities

1. **Modern dataset evidence:** opt-in versioned physical/logical bindings for structured selectors and partitioned inputs, plus verified Workbench publication. Preserve v1 bytes and source identity algorithms.
2. **Complete suite authoring:** explicitly compose ordered suite-v2 cases, tags, limits, execution settings and policy locks; validate authoritatively and export unapproved drafts with destination-relative references.
3. **Recoverable suites:** explicit retained workspace, authoritative reuse preview and deliberate restart/resume with one job. Prove eligible reuse and stale-input non-reuse.
4. **Policy-change review:** two explicit recipes and an evolution declaration, shared deterministic assessment, summary-first disclosure, and no bypass of exact-policy evidence comparison. Draft generation is optional supporting work, not a separate feature.
5. **Suite rehearsal series:** bounded, caller-ordered, same-policy analysis of complete verified suites with case-scoped discrepancy transitions and input-identity changes; version the artifact and verifier.
6. **Complete reconciliation workflow:** reviewed local declarations/input/receipt binding, execution, cancellation, verified history/results, preserved-evidence failure diagnosis, and explicitly versioned reconciliation/dataset-evidence handoff support. This is one capability group, not separate feature credit for screens or exports.

Use the original research gates where applicable, amended by the blind review. Freeze a small contract and acceptance fixtures for each capability before implementation. Qualify reconciliation count shapes, arithmetic relationships and outcome derivation before building stronger diagnostic claims. Confirm the finding with targeted tests; do not describe source review as a reproduced exploit. Existing Statement/report exporters cannot be assumed to support reconciliation or dataset-evidence layouts.

## Deferred

Defer strict reconciliation comparison, standalone dataset assertions and evidence archives. Fixed alignment identity binds window and receipt, so comparison does not span changed extraction contexts. Assertions need a coherent composition contract. Archive ingestion adds a hostile-input surface without being necessary to complete this train.
